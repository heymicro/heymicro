Privacy.

This page covers heymicro.org, developed and operated by Micro XYZ LTD. Using the hosted assistant at micro.mu is covered by its separate privacy policy.

Visiting this website

These are static pages. They contain no advertising, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and the website code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Hosting infrastructure may record requests, including IP addresses, to serve the site and handle errors or abuse.

Sending feedback

The feedback form sends your message and, if you provide it, your email address to Micro’s Forms service at micro.mu. No account is required. We use this information to understand your feedback and reply when appropriate.

Responses are stored privately in the operator’s account; they are not published with the form. Submitting feedback does not automatically send it to an AI model or subscribe you to a mailing list. Your IP address is used for submission rate limits and may appear in operational logs.

Keeping and deleting feedback

Feedback remains stored until the operator deletes it; the form has no automatic expiry. To request access, correction or deletion, email admin@micro.mu. Include enough detail to identify your submission. We may need to verify that it belongs to you.

Other services

Links to GitHub and micro.mu take you to those services. Installing Micro downloads software from the micro/mu repository and GitHub releases. A self-hosted instance is operated by the person running it, with its own configuration and providers.
